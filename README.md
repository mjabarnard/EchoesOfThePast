# Echoes of the Past: Scarborough's Lost Aquarium

An interactive web exhibition exploring the history of Scarborough's Aquarium/People's Palace/Gala Land through ambisonic audio and archival imagery.

## About

What happens when a building that holds meaning for a community is demolished? Is it gone without a trace, or does it leave echoes for future generations? This project uses three-dimensional recording technology to evoke a sense of place, combining the acoustic scale of the existing South Bay car park with the grand Victorian arcade at Brighton SEA LIFE Centre to tell Scarborough's Aquarium story as if heard in the building itself.

## The Five Locations

1. **Entrance Court** - The grand entrance as part of the design by Eugenius Birch
2. **Central Arcade** - Victorian tanks among Indian-inspired arches
3. **The Stage** - Entertainment and music hall performances
4. **Underwater Feats** - Captain Matthew Webb's legendary swim & Ada Webb's prowess in the water
5. **The Grotto** - Picturesque rock walls and the tram crash of 1925
